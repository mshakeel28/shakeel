SERS for biofilm detection

Biofilm-forming strains of Staphylococcus epidermidis are clinically important and hard to tell apart quickly. Surface-enhanced Raman spectra, fed through chemometric classification, separate them from non-forming strains.

Role
First author — measurement, analysis
Period
2020 – 2022
Techniques
SERS, silver nanoparticles, PCA, PLS-DA
Institution
University of Agriculture Faisalabad

The problem

Biofilm formation changes how bacteria respond to treatment, so distinguishing forming from non-forming strains matters clinically. Conventional assays are slow. Raman scattering reports on molecular composition directly, but the signal from bacterial pellets is weak.

Approach

Silver nanoparticles provide the surface enhancement that makes the measurement practical on bacterial pellets. The resulting spectra differ in ways too subtle to read by eye, so principal component analysis reduced them and PLS-DA built the classifier.
