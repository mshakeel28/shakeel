Antimicrobial peptides in lipid bilayers

MelP5 punches pores in lipid membranes, but bulk measurements average over every stage of the process. A site-specific IR label reports on one residue at a time, so the insertion can be followed piece by piece.

Role
Sample preparation, measurement, analysis
Period
2024 – present
Techniques
FTIR, 2D IR, site-specific IR labels
System
MelP5 in POPC bilayers

The question

MelP5 is an engineered variant of melittin that forms large, stable pores in lipid bilayers. What the peptide does to the membrane — and what the membrane does back to the peptide — depends on where along the sequence you look and on the physical state of the lipid, which changes sharply with temperature.

Approach

Site-specific IR labels sit in an uncrowded region of the spectrum, so a single label gives a clean, assignable band whose centre frequency and lineshape track its local electrostatics and hydration. Measurements across a temperature range let the lipid phase behaviour act as a variable rather than a nuisance.
